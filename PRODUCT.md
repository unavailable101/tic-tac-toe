# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players starting a quick game with a friend on the same device or playing solo against the computer.

## Product Purpose

Play three in a row with your chosen emoji marks and see each mode’s score across rounds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Local two-player play and three computer difficulty levels.
- Player marks come from a shared emoji library. The computer uses a robot mark.
- Light and dark themes are available. The selected theme persists on the device.
- Rounds, emoji selections, and scores are session state.
